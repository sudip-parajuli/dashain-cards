## Full description

Good cards. Better company.

Bring your Dashain card table with you. Dashain Cards brings together Marriage, Call Break, Dhumbal, Dahar Mara, Kitti, Faras and Jutpatti, with English and Nepali interfaces.

PLAY YOUR WAY

Play offline with computer opponents, or create a friend room and share its six-digit code. Friends can join from different devices over Wi-Fi or mobile data. Supported player counts and house variants differ by game.

KEEP THE FAMILY TABLE IN SYNC

Keep records for your physical card games, with running points and amounts. Marriage round details explain each player's calculation. Share a live score link so friends can follow the record on their own devices. The owner controls edits and can transfer ownership.

MAKE THE TABLE YOUR OWN

Adjust supported game rules, point values and table settings. Sort and move your cards, rotate your phone for a wider table, and control music and card sounds in Settings.

VIRTUAL COINS ONLY

Coins and chips are for entertainment. They cannot be purchased, cashed out or exchanged for money. Faras includes simulated betting with virtual chips. This app does not offer real-money gambling.

No player account is required. Offline records are stored on your device. Optional online games and shared scorekeepers use our game service; see the privacy policy for details. Local coins and saved progress do not sync between devices. Not every regional variation is supported.
