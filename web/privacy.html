Offline play

Names, scores, house rules, saved games, virtual coin balances, reward timestamps and language and sound preferences are stored on your device. Offline play does not send these records to a server. Backups and exports may include names and scores; you choose where to save or share them.

Playing with friends

Multiplayer is optional and supports Marriage, Call Break, Dhumbal, Dahar Mara, Kitti, Faras and Jutpatti. Our Cloudflare service generates and stores the deck, hands, room codes, rules, names, seat credentials, moves, scores, table chip balances and the host’s chip-value setting. Names are optional; unnamed players receive a generic name. Each device receives only its own concealed cards and public table information. Hands are revealed when a deal finishes. In Faras, your blind cards are withheld from your own device until you choose to look. Kitti splits remain private until every player completes their split.

Rooms are stored using Cloudflare Durable Objects, with new rooms processed separately. Existing rooms migrate without changing their codes or seat credentials. Real-time connections deliver only the state a viewer is authorized to see; polling is a fallback. Rooms expire after six idle hours and are removed during hourly cleanup, normally within seven idle hours. An active room remains available while players use it. Unlike the local development server, restarting a Worker does not erase a saved room. A private credential saved on your device lets you resume your seat.

Shared score records

Sharing a scorekeeper uploads that table’s game, player names, rules, round notes, points and amounts to Cloudflare. People with its six-digit score code can join as read-only viewers. The owner can edit the record and transfer ownership to a joined viewer; the previous owner then becomes read-only. Private credentials authorize your session and are stored on your device and our service; live connection attachments may retain them while connected. They are never included in score backups or sent to other viewers. Shared records survive server restarts and expire after 30 days without viewing or editing. Clearing local storage removes access credentials; save or transfer ownership first if needed. Score sharing does not upload your wallet, unrelated tables or hidden cards.

Connection data and providers

Online connections use HTTPS. Cloudflare processes network information such as IP addresses; the service uses IP addresses in temporary memory for request limits. Session credentials are sent in authorization headers or WebSocket handshake headers, not share links. Application observability logging is disabled. Cloudflare may retain operational data under its own policies. When you use the company website address, Vercel also routes the connection. See Cloudflare privacy and Vercel privacy.

Permissions and deletion

The Android app uses Internet permission for multiplayer. It does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access. No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, purchases or cash gambling are included. Chips are virtual.

Delete local scoreboards in History or clear offline records in Settings → Data & backups. The Settings reset clears scores, saved games and settings while preserving your coin balance. Leaving an active game forfeits its committed table budget or boot; Faras forfeits chips already in the pot. Coin balances and gifts are local to this device, are not backed up with score exports and do not sync between devices. Clearing browser/app storage or uninstalling removes the local wallet, preferences and room credentials. Exported files remain where you saved them. Clearing your device does not immediately delete server rooms; they expire after inactivity. There are no player accounts. Contact our support email about stored room data, giving the room code but never your private seat credential.
